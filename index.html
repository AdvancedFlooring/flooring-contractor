FLOORING EXPERT ADVICE

5 Ways to Keep Your York, PA Floors Beautiful All Year

Advice from Advanced Flooring Inc — Serving York, PA & surrounding areas

Living in Pennsylvania means dealing with humid summers and snowy winters. These temperature changes can affect your hardwood. Here’s what we tell every one of our customers to help their floors stay strong through every season.

1. Maintain Consistent Humidity

Wood expands and contracts with moisture. During York winters, using a humidifier can prevent gaps from forming between your hardwood planks when the air gets dry.

2. Use the Right Cleaning Products

Avoid harsh chemicals or excessive water. A damp (not dripping) microfiber mop with a pH-neutral cleaner is the best way to preserve the finish of your floors.

3. Use Rugs in High-Traffic Areas

Place mats at every entrance to catch salt, grit, and moisture before they reach your floors. This simple step prevents micro-scratches on your hardwood finish.

4. Trim Your Pet's Nails

Large pets can scratch even the toughest finishes over time. Keeping nails trimmed helps prevent deep gouges that might require a full sand and refinish later.

5. Call a Professional for Deep Scratches

Minor scuffs are normal, but deep scratches that reach the bare wood need professional sealing to prevent water damage. This is not a DIY project — use a trained crew.
